# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack
Flask and Jinja templates. Tailwind CSS for styling.

## Users
Users looking for new and used car recommendations based on specific filters.

## Product Purpose
A car recommendation tool with filters and results display. Helps users discover cars that match their criteria.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Jinja templating structure and existing Flask routes/endpoints must remain intact.
- Only markup, CSS, and static assets may be modified.
- Form fields, filter inputs, and data bindings must be preserved exactly (no renaming or restructuring of data).
