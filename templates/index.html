How it works

No filters to fiddle with. You talk, we do the sorting.

  1. Describe your car

    Budget, body style, fuel, gearbox, seats, features: say as much or as little as you like.

  2. We understand English and Hinglish

    "Sunroof wali SUV 12 lakh ke andar" works just as well as "SUV with a sunroof under 12 lakh".

  3. We check 832 current variants

    Every match comes from a verified knowledge base of new cars on sale in India, filtered strictly against what you asked for.

  4. You see matches, honestly labelled

    Each car shows why it fits. When a spec isn't verified yet, we say so instead of guessing.

For developers: a Flask app with an ML + regex intent parser, hard filters and a ranking engine over an SQLite knowledge base with per-field provenance. Read the code on GitHub.

Car images are taken from manufacturer brochures. Prices are ex-showroom; check on-road prices before you buy.
