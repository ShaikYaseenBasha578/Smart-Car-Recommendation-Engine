  • Expert scores are dated. Autocar India scores were published between 11 Jun 2025 and 29 Oct 2025. A model changed after its review may be scored on the earlier car.expert score rows
  • Some owner themes rest on very few reviews. 42 of 271 owner theme rows rest on 3 or fewer mentions, and 89 on fewer than 5, out of a sample of reviews per model.owner theme table
  • An independent spot check found errors. A reviewer judged 40 stored values from memory, not from a source lookup: 24 looked right, 6 looked wrong and 10 were doubtful. The reviewer saw the errors cluster in fields that rest on the import alone and in recently added models.independent audit file
